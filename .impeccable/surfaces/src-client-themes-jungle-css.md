# I'm a Celebrity world

Scope: /im-a-celeb show page, join screen, room, end screens and home door. Mode: Operate (the room). Users watch on November nights, often nightly. The user's red line is "hard to tap in the dark". The user delegated the direction choice ("pick for me"), so the roll's assigned direction was built without a choice round.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The bingo card is a camp board of carved wooden planks. Marking a square brands it: the plank chars and a Bushtucker star burns in. This refuses the category default of a green jungle-leaf skin with emoji bugs.

OWN-WORLD:
- Jungle-night ground (#0c1710) with a warm torchlight vignette from above.
- Planks are cedar (#a8743d) with grain, hard char (#2e1b0e) edges, routed dark lettering and rope lashings.
- Torch orange (#ff8a1f) and ember (#ffc46b) are the light.
- Rye (wood type) is the display face; a system sans carries reading text.
- State is never colour alone: a marked plank is charred with a burned star, a winning line gets gold stars and a gold edge, and the plank that would finish a line gets a dashed torch-orange rope edge.

STORY: A campmate joins ("Welcome to camp!"), marks planks as the clichés land, and each mark brands a star in. Calling LINE flares every branded plank at once.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone room:
- A compact header (back link, episode, closing time, code and invite).
- Standard tabs.
- A full-width 5×5 plank board with the "Bushtucker · FREE" centre.
- Torch-orange calls.

Desktop at 1200px and wider: a camp-board sidebar (a plank header and a charred tally of "stars earned"), the board, then chat and people.

FORM: Torchlit camp signage, candidate 6 of my ordered list; seed key 9618d3c9. Signature interaction: branding (a brightness flare, then the star scales in). Motion: one flare per mark, a whole-board flare on a claim, nothing loops, and reduced motion leaves instant states only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
